Changes for Perfect Pitch:
Selected 29 velocity-8 recordings (C1 through C8 in minor thirds); resampled to
44.1 kHz stereo; removed leading near-silence with a short attack margin;
corrected estimated pitch toward equal temperament (A4 = 440 Hz) to remove
the original piano's stretched tuning; balanced register levels with bounded
attack-level normalization; trimmed each to two seconds with a
100 ms end fade; joined in 2.1-second slots;
re-encoded as one MP3 bank for efficient loading. Browser playback transposes
the nearest recording by at most one semitone, with gain and release envelopes.
No endorsement by the original creators is implied.

The tuning report is piano-tuning.json. Low notes use the second partial as a
pitch reference because their fundamental is weak. Real string resonances and
small changes in pitch during each recorded decay are intentionally retained.
